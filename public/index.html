The problem

India's welfare system is vast and genuinely generous — scholarships, pensions, subsidies, insurance, housing and livelihood support exist for almost every walk of life. But that scale is also the problem: schemes are scattered across hundreds of department websites, written in dense official language, and next to impossible to search by "does this apply to me?"

The result is one of the country's quieter inequities: the people who most need a scheme are often the least likely to hear about it.

What Sarkar Sahayak does

  • 01Pulls together scheme data from central and state government sources into one searchable place.
  • 02Turns dense eligibility text into a short set of plain-language questions.
  • 03Shows you the benefit, the eligibility criteria, the documents and the exact application steps — no jargon detour.
  • 04Lets you share a scheme straight to family or WhatsApp groups, because the best distribution channel for welfare is word of mouth.

What this isn't

Sarkar Sahayak is a discovery tool, not a government office. It doesn't submit applications on your behalf and eligibility matching is a best-effort read of each scheme's published rules. Always verify details on the official portal linked in each scheme's application steps.
